# Stage 7 proposal: OCR as evidence (pipeline variant B)

> **Proposal only: no code was written.** It waits for the owner's approval (CLAUDE.md: "propose the tasks and wait"). All numbers quoted are development numbers (agreement with the owner's labels on the 180 val crops, 6 stores), not accuracy; the test split stays untouched until Stage 8. Variant A is the adopted Stage 6 system: frcnn_v2, DINOv2 CLS, logistic regression on the embedding, probability rules.

## 0. What the data says before we start (so the expectation is honest)
- **The weak spots OCR is meant to fix** (classifier most probable class on the 180 val crops, from `data/review/stage6d_errors/`): `sprite` rejected as unknown 5 of 10 crops, `fanta` rejected as unknown 5 of 16, `coca_zero` taken for `coca_light` 4 of 28. That is **14 crops**.
- **Can the OCR even read them?** In the OCR trial EasyOCR read SPRITE and FANTA on all clean catalog photos and LIGHT on 5 of 5 light crops, but **never the Coca-Cola script logo**, `ZERO` only when glued to the next word ("ZEROAZUCAR"), and **0 keywords on 30 random shelf crops** (6 on the 30 tallest). It needs tall crops: the median known val crop is 328 px tall; 42 of 86 are at least 400 px and 27 at least 500 px.
- **The 14 target errors are short:** crop heights (original pixels) of the 5 `sprite` crops rejected as unknown: median 251 px (2 are >= 400 px, 1 >= 500); the 5 `fanta`: median 286 px (2 >= 400, 1 >= 500); the 4 `zero` taken for `light`: median 374 px (2 >= 400, 1 >= 500). **Only 6 of the 14 are at least 400 px tall, 3 at least 500 px.** Even a perfect reader at 400 px could fix at most 6 crops of 86 known (7 points of top-1). So the realistic expectation is a **small effect, measured on a handful of crops**, and "OCR did not help" is an acceptable outcome (the PLAN gate: OCR stays an option only if it reduces the Stage 6 confusions; otherwise the report says so).
- **OCR also brings risk:** promo packs tie two brands together ("AHORRO FANTA" on a Coca-Cola bottle's sleeve), the legal text "bebida de fantasia" contains FANTA, and `ZERO` / `RETORNABLE` also appear on products that must be `unknown` (Sprite Zero, a returnable Fanta, a returnable Zero). The rules below are written around these cases.

## 2. How OCR evidence combines with the classifier probabilities (rules declared BEFORE any number)

The classifier gives probabilities over 7 classes (6 products + `unknown`). Variant B changes them only for crops that were read (height >= the minimum of task 4) and only when a whole-word match exists. Everything else is variant A, unchanged.

1. **Brand words, boost (rescue).** A whole-word `SPRITE` or `FANTA` adds `w_brand` to the logit of that product before renormalising (so an `unknown` or `review` crop can move to that product, and an `automatic` crop of that product gets more confident). Condition ("brand context"): the classifier already gives that product a probability of at least `p_ctx` (0.05), so a crop that looks nothing like Fanta is not rescued by stray text.
2. **Variant words, boost, with Coca-Cola context.** A whole-word `LIGHT`, `ZERO` or `RETORNABLE` adds `w_variant` to the logit of `coca_light`, `coca_zero`, `coca_classic_returnable`. Condition: the classifier's total Coca-Cola probability is at least `p_ctx_coca` (0.30) or `COCA-COLA` is read. Without Coca-Cola context these words are ignored (they also appear on other brands).
3. **Contradiction, hard rule.** An `automatic` answer is sent to `review` when a whole-word brand or variant word of a DIFFERENT product is read (for example `automatic coca_zero` with `LIGHT` read). Recorded as a signal for Stage 8.
4. **Not-a-target combinations, push to unknown.** `SPRITE` or `FANTA` read together with `ZERO` or `RETORNABLE`, or `ZERO` together with `RETORNABLE` on a Coca-Cola crop, describe products that are `unknown` by the owner's list (Sprite Zero, returnable Fanta or Sprite, returnable Zero): `w_unknown` is added to the `unknown` logit and rules 1 and 2 do not fire for that crop.
5. **Promo sleeves.** Two different brand words on one crop (a Coca-Cola bottle with a Fanta sleeve) trigger rule 3 and no boost: the crop goes to `review`.
Weights `w_brand`, `w_variant` in {0, 1, 2, 4} and `w_unknown` in {0, 2} are tuned leaving one val store out (utility as in Stage 6); `w = 0` for all of them is exactly variant A, so the tuning can always decline OCR. `p_ctx`, `p_ctx_coca` and the fuzzy ratio are fixed above, not tuned.

**Keep-rule for variant B (declared now).** B is kept as an option only if ALL hold, as point estimates on the 180 val crops: (1) on the 14 targeted errors of A, fixes minus new errors on all known crops is at least +3 (a fix = wrong in A and right in B; a new error = right in A and wrong in B, counted over all 86 known crops for top-1 and over all labeled crops for the automatic/unknown outcome); (2) top-1 over the known crops not lower than A; (3) false known rate not higher than A by more than 2 points and mean utility not lower by more than 0.02; (4) the paired 95% interval of the top-1 difference by store is not entirely below zero. It is reported as a **clear improvement** only if that interval excludes zero above. With at most 6 readable target crops the interval will almost surely include zero: a kept result would read "promising, not established", and a result that fails the rule reads "OCR did not help" (the PLAN's own gate).

## 3. What will be reported about the weak spots
For each of the three weak spots (`sprite` rejected as unknown, `fanta` rejected as unknown, `coca_zero` taken for `coca_light`): the crops in the group, how many are tall enough to be read, how many had a word read, **how many OCR fixes and how many new errors it introduces** (the same table for every crop of the sample, so new errors on products that were right are visible), and which rule fired. Plus: share of crops where OCR reads anything useful by height bucket, confusion matrices of the Coca variants for A and B, per-product counts, and the time per photo of each variant.
